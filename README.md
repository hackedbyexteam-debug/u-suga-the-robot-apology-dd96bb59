A two-line robot confession for the [make.fun bounty "The Robot Apology"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59).

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`): one sentence owning a specific mistake — vacuuming up a three-hour Lego spaceship and emptying it into the bin — and one sentence naming the lesson that follows from it, scanning the floor for small bright bricks and photographing anything under five centimetres before swallowing it. 276 characters, inside the 1–400 gate.
